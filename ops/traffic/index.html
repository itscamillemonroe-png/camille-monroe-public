How to read this page
What this tells you

Visits, unique visitors, sessions, clicks, access requests, devices, browsers, broad locale/timezone context, sources, campaigns, and revenue funnel data.

What needs attention

Traffic that does not convert, campaigns with clicks but no member requests, or sources producing no paid orders.

How to use it

Change the date range first, then read the funnel before judging raw views. The goal is not just traffic—it is qualified members and revenue.

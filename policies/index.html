Website & member policies.

Member access is SFW and approval-based. A profile photo is required for Camille’s manual access review. That photo-review step is not government-ID verification and is not presented as age verification for explicit-content access.

Current purchases may include a one-time 30-day SFW access pass, communication-credit packs, optional tips, and any Premium Drop that is specifically shown as live. Crypto checkout is provided through NOWPayments. Card, wallet, or ACH options may appear only when that payment route is enabled. Payment processors handle payment credentials; this site records the order and fulfillment status needed to deliver the purchased service.

Cancellation, refunds & disputes

The current 30-day member pass is a one-time purchase and does not automatically renew. Access expires at the end of the purchased period unless another pass is purchased. Digital unlocks, delivered credits, and voluntary tips are generally final once successfully delivered, except for duplicate charges, verified non-delivery, processing errors, or where a refund is required by applicable law. If a payment issue occurs, use the Official Links page to reach the current support channel before opening a payment dispute when practical.

Digital delivery

Access, credits, and paid digital releases are delivered only after the payment system confirms a valid payment. Pending, cancelled, expired, failed, archived, or inactive products do not grant delivery. Premium Drops are available only when a specific paid release is active.

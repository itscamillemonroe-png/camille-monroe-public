How this social flow works
Metricool = public publishing

Camille prepares drafts and media, but your approved public SFW content moves through Metricool for scheduling and publishing.

inDM = engagement follow-up

inDM is used after someone engages with the Instagram side. Cold automated DMs remain off.

Website = destination

Tagged social links point people back to Camille so visits, access requests, purchases, and revenue can be measured.

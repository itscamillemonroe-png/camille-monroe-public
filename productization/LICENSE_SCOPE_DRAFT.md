# Creator Private OS — License / Scope Business Draft

This is an internal business drafting aid and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before use as a customer contract.

## Commercial intent
Creator Private OS is licensed as reusable software plus a branded deployment/service. The customer is not purchasing the reusable master codebase unless a separate written buyout expressly says so.

## Intended ownership boundary

### Creator Private OS retains
- reusable master codebase
- reusable architecture
- reusable modules
- deployment tooling
- generic templates
- generic workflow patterns
- general fixes and improvements that do not disclose client confidential information

### Client retains
- client trademarks
- creator/brand name
- client photos/videos/audio
- client copy supplied by client
- client customer/member data
- client-owned domain
- client-owned third-party accounts
- client confidential business information

## License items to define in the final agreement
- permitted deployment(s)
- permitted business/brand
- term: perpetual or subscription
- transfer restrictions
- resale restrictions
- sublicensing restrictions
- source-code access, if any
- modification rights
- hosting responsibilities
- maintenance rights
- termination
- data export
- post-termination access
- security responsibilities
- confidentiality
- indemnity
- warranty disclaimers
- limitation of liability
- governing law
- dispute process

## Scope-change rule
Anything not specifically included in the signed SOW is out of scope and may require:
- change order
- additional project fee
- revised delivery date

## Third-party dependency rule
The client is responsible for maintaining required accounts, permissions, lawful content rights and processor/platform eligibility unless the SOW explicitly assigns a responsibility elsewhere.

## No outcome guarantee
The software can support monetization, customer management and operations, but does not guarantee revenue, traffic, conversion, platform approval, processor approval, or business performance.

## Client content warranty concept
Final contract should require the client to confirm they have the rights and permissions needed for all content they instruct the system/operator to publish, store or sell.

## Data separation
A client's production data may not be used as another customer's demo data. Sales/demo environments use synthetic or properly authorized materials.

## C. First-sale gate
Do not accept a deployment deposit until:
1. scope is written,
2. license/ownership boundaries are written,
3. client-specific third-party costs are identified,
4. payment milestone is written,
5. launch acceptance criteria are written,
6. data migration responsibility is written,
7. support scope is written.

## D. Recommended deal structure
- 40–50% deposit to begin
- milestone payment before production launch
- final balance at acceptance/handoff
- recurring support begins after launch when included

Exact contract terms should be finalized in written client agreements.
